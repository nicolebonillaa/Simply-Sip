Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Simply Sip respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name or username, email address, and password. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text.
  • Location: your device location, only if you grant permission. We use it to show drinks and cafés near you and to display the map.
  • Dietary and preference information: dietary restrictions, allergies, and calorie, sugar, ingredient, and taste preferences you choose to enter. This is sensitive, and we use it only to filter and recommend drinks.
  • Content you create: favorites, ratings, reviews, and comments.
  • Technical information: device type, operating system, app version, IP address, and crash or error logs.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To filter drinks, show nearby locations, and make recommendations.
  • To save your favorites and display your ratings and reviews.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the app.
  • To respond to your questions and feedback.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your dietary, allergy, or location information for advertising.

3. How we share information

We share information only in these cases:

  • Service providers that help us run the app, such as hosting, database, and crash-reporting services, and mapping services from Apple or Google. They may only use the information to provide their services.
  • Other users, for content you choose to post. Ratings and reviews may be visible to other users under your username.
  • Legal reasons, if required by law or to protect the safety and rights of our users and team.

Your dietary and allergy settings and your exact location are not shown to other users.

4. Location

We request location access only to show nearby results. You can deny or turn off location access at any time in your device settings. The app will still work, but nearby results and the map will be limited. We do not track your location in the background. We use it only while you are using the app.

5. Data security

We use reasonable safeguards, including hashed passwords, token-based sign-in, and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app or by emailing us. When you do, we delete your profile, preferences, and favorites within 30 days, and we delete or anonymize your reviews. We may keep limited records if the law requires it.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and update your account and preferences in the app, withdraw location permission at any time, and ask us to access, correct, or delete your information. If you live in California, you also have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request its deletion, and to opt out of its sale. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, email [team email address].

8. Age requirement

Simply Sip is intended for users 18 and older, such as CSUN students. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Third-party services

The app uses map and location services provided by Apple and Google, which have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the practices of third-party services.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make significant changes, we will notify you in the app and update the date at the top.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email [team email address].
